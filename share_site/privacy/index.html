Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

This policy explains what OlympusGO collects, why, who sees it and the choices you have. It follows Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act and the data protection laws of the other countries we operate in.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your name, username, email, mobile number, birthday and country. Your birthday is used to apply age rules and can’t be changed once set.
  • Sports profile: your sports, skill levels, who you like to play with, and your gear if you add it.
  • Activity: the Meets, tournaments, events, classes and clubs you join or run, attendance, scores and ratings.
  • Messages and posts: chats, club announcements, photos, videos and reviews you send.
  • Payments: what you paid, refunds and payouts. Card details are handled by our payment provider; we don’t store full card numbers.
  • Location: used for nearby results and check-in at a venue, only when you allow it.
  • Device and usage: app version, device type, crash reports and basic usage, to keep the app working and secure.

2. How we use it

  • To run your account and show you Meets, clubs and players that fit your level and area.
  • To take payments, pay organizers and sellers, and handle refunds.
  • To calculate ratings and leaderboards from confirmed results.
  • To keep players safe: reviewing reports, preventing fraud and protecting under-18s.
  • To send sign-in codes, reminders and updates you have asked for. You can turn notifications off in Settings.

3. Who sees what

  • Your name, username, photo, sports and level are visible to signed-in players.
  • You choose who sees your sessions: everyone, your Circle, or nobody.
  • Your email, mobile number, birthday and location are never shown to other players.
  • Organizers see the details they need to run their session, such as who is coming and attendance.
  • Club and team chats are visible only to their members.

4. Under-18s

  • Players from 13 to 17 need a parent or guardian’s approval, and the parent can see their sessions and payments.
  • Under-18 profiles are private, can’t receive private messages from adults, and show initials on public leaderboards.
  • Filming an under-18 needs a parent’s consent.

5. Who we share it with

  • Service providers who run parts of OlympusGO for us, such as hosting and database (Supabase), text messages (Twilio) and payments. They may only use your data to provide their service.
  • Authorities when the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.
  • We don’t sell your personal data.

6. How long we keep it

  • We keep your data while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account, we remove your profile. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires, and ratings history is anonymised.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can see and change most of your details in Account Centre, and download or delete your account there.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to stop using it for a purpose. Use Help in the app.
  • If you aren’t happy with our answer, you can contact the data protection authority in your country.

8. Security

Your data is stored with access controls so that people only see what they’re allowed to. Sign-in uses one-time codes and Face ID on your device. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your devices safe and tell us if you notice anything wrong.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. If a change is important, we’ll tell you in the app before it takes effect.

10. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Use Help in the app to contact us.
