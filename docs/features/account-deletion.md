# Account deletion

**What it does.** A player deletes their account in the app (Apple guideline 5.1.1(v)). The account closes at once, the money they are owed comes back, and they have `account.delete_undo_days` (30) to log in and keep it. Then a daily job erases their private data and leaves "Former player" on the results they were part of.

**Files.** `supabase/migrations/20261112090000_account_deletion.sql`, `supabase/tests/54_account_deletion_test.sql`, `supabase/tests/races/deletion_races.sh`, `lib/features/settings/presentation/account_screens.dart` (`DeleteAccountScreen`, `AccountClosingScreen`), `lib/features/settings/data/settings_repository.dart`, `test/account_deletion_test.dart`, `test/supabase_deletion_local_test.dart`.

**The auth.users row is never deleted.** Every table keys on it, and a hard delete would take a child's records, a voucher's order and a match result with it (Q139). Keeping the row means nothing cascades; the erase job deletes exactly what is private and leaves a tombstone. The wrong cascades were corrected anyway: an academy owner or Meet host cannot be hard-deleted under their rows, and a child's enrolment, a support request, an order's voucher link and a report's reporter survive one.

**Steps.**

1. **Request** (`account_delete_request`, after Face ID). Refused with a plain message while the caller owns a club, team, open academy or active shop, is staff, or has earnings to be paid out (R-ACC-15, Q238). Otherwise: a row in `account_deletions`, `profiles.hidden_at` set (remembering whether moderation had hidden them), Meets they host that have not started are cancelled through `cancel_activity` so their players are refunded, everything they paid for an activity still to come is refunded in full with the fee under key `account_deleted:<payment>`, paid lessons still to come are cancelled and refunded, and an Inbox note says how to undo. Shop orders finish on their own. Asking twice returns the same date.
2. **Closing.** `private.gate_request` refuses a closing caller every API path except `private.closing_allow`: their own status, keeping the account, help. Hidden means discovery, leaderboards, the feed and other players' profile reads leave them out. The app signs out locally; the saved tile stays, because logging in from it is how they come back. On every sign-in the router sends a closing account to `AccountClosingScreen`, which offers "Keep my account" (`account_delete_cancel`, which unhides unless moderation hid them) or "Log out".
3. **Erase** (`private.account_erase_due`, pg_cron `retention-delete`, daily 18:00 UTC). Takes requests past `erase_after` that were not cancelled, and skips any with money still moving (an open Shop order or lesson, an open dispute, a held payment, a refund not yet settled), recording `blocked_reason`; after `account.erase_blocked_alert_days` (7) a `money_alerts` row tells staff. `private.account_erase` then queues avatar, chat-photo and licence files, clears contacts and birthday, deletes sports, settings, follows, blocks, saved items, messages, memberships, carts, reviews, coach rows, codes, locations, device tokens, ratings, leaderboard rows, games, workouts, plans, clips and posts, forfeits open points lots, lapses vouchers, ends guardian links (a child with no other linked adult is restricted again, records intact; `academy_students.parent_id` goes null), tombstones the profile (`full_name = 'Former player'`, `erased_at`), and bans the Supabase Auth user where the role may. Payments, orders, refunds, the ledger, payouts, points entries, match results, rating events, reports, cases, audit rows, assessments and consents stay.

4. **Audit kept 10 years** (`private.account_audit_purge_due`, pg_cron `retention-audit`, daily 18:30 UTC; `supabase/migrations/20261114090000_deletion_audit_retention.sql`). `account.audit_keep_years` (10) after `erased_at`, the job removes the account's rows in `admin_audit` (acted, or `target_kind` `user` or `licence` about it, except staff decisions that moved money or points), `attendance_audit` (acted; in someone else's roster change only its own line is taken out), `club_audit` and `catalog_audit` (acted), then its `account_deletions` row. A cancelled request is kept for ever, and a purged account can't ask again (`account_deletions_not_erased` checks `profiles.erased_at`). The three append-only audit tables use `private.forbid_audit_change`: a delete (or, on `attendance_audit`, the roster update) passes only while `og.audit_purge` is on and the session runs as the job's owner; players hold no write grant on them. Money, points and results keep `private.forbid_change`. Review fixes in `20261114100000_deletion_audit_review_fixes.sql`. What stays and why is in Q264.

**Rules and numbers.** `account.delete_undo_days`, `account.erase_blocked_alert_days`, `account.audit_keep_years` in `app_settings`. R-ACC-11, R-ACC-12, R-ACC-14, R-ACC-15, R-REF-18.

**Tests.** pgTAP file 55 (36 checks: kept a day short of 10 years from the erase even when the erase was due earlier, removed a day after, a cancelled request kept for ever whatever its dates, staff decisions on refunds, points and held money kept, a roster change keeping the other players' lines, the tombstone and the bystander's rows kept, a purged account refused a new request, the window read from the setting, deletes, updates and truncates refused outside the job, service_role refused even with the allowance on, no player write grants, only the three audit tables on the new guard, no client can run it). pgTAP file 54 (86 checks: refusals, refunds, idempotency, the gate, undo, a moderation hide kept, the erase and what it leaves, a 12-year-old left restricted with records intact while a child with a second parent carries on, the blocked erase and its alert, hard-delete defences). `deletion_races.sh`: parallel requests give one refund and one request. Widget tests for both screens; `supabase_deletion_local_test.dart` through the real API (hidden, refused, kept). Walked through on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator against the local stack on 8 Nov 2026 as the seed buyer: Account Centre (Face ID) → Delete account → Delete with Face ID → Closing screen with the 8 Dec date → Done logs out; the saved tile logs back in to the closing gate; Keep my account returns Home with `cancelled_at` set, `hidden_at` cleared and an Inbox notice "Your account is closing".

**Known gaps.** UAT of 8 Nov 2026 (`docs/audits/2026-11-08/REPORT.md`) found two release blockers, fixed the same day: the earnings refusal now uses `private.organizer_net` per currency, and `private.account_erase` re-checks the request under its lock so an undo always wins. The medium and low findings listed there are still open; a deleting organizer's fee debt is not refused (Q245). No email or SMS notice of the request (Q239). A parent cannot delete a child's account (Q240). Messages are deleted outright (Q241). A coach's upcoming one-to-ones stand (Q242). `banned_until` and the session deletes on `auth.*` are tried and skipped on insufficient privilege; the gate and the empty contacts are what stop a sign-in. Apple's acceptance of the flow is not proven until review. The 'Former player' fallback relies on `private.masked_name` and `private.public_name`; older functions that read `profiles.full_name` directly show "Former player" too, but ones that coalesce to 'Player' for a missing row were not all audited.
